Metabolic health isn’t static. Glucose levels change throughout the day. Unlike traditional studies that rely solely on fasting labs, this trial incorporated three full days of continuous glucose monitoring at baseline and post-intervention — capturing real-world glucose exposure, peaks, and variability.
