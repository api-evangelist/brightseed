Plants produce compounds that can not only affect our biology when we consume them but also when we smell them. Aromatherapy is the practice of using fragrances—usually from plants—to modulate emotions and general well-being. It’s part art and part science, but the knowledge of plants’ powerful aromas is as old as the first civilizations.
